Written 2026-09-15. Two commits: 0d26dc2 (re-com-video), d44c1ee (simkl-mcp).


=============================================================================
PLAN VERIFICATION
=============================================================================

Re-checked the load-bearing claims against primary sources. The plan holds up
— Simkl's PIN flow, rate limits, statuses, ID uniqueness rules and
don't-follow-the-redirect are all as written; Wikidata is live, CC0, and joins
cleanly on IMDb ID.

Three corrections / additions:

* §7.1 missed two endpoints.
  - POST /sync/watched gives a *server-side* per-item answer to "is this
    already in the library?" That's the independent check that makes the
    exclusion guarantee verifiable rather than merely believed, since the
    local mirror is only as fresh as the last sync.
  - GET /sync/ratings/{type}/{rating} reads ratings back without pulling the
    whole library.

* PIN device_code is the literal string "DEVICE_CODE" — a placeholder.
  Polling uses user_code, and must stop at the first token (Simkl deletes an
  approved code; polling an unknown one issues a *fresh* one, so a naive
  client waits forever).

* WDQS returns transient 502s — hit it on the first live run. Retried,
  bounded. A 429 and a query timeout are deliberately NOT retried.


=============================================================================
WHAT GOT BUILT
=============================================================================

simkl-mcp  (new sibling repo, Phase 1)
  14 tools, 84 unit tests, no network. Built around Simkl's *quiet* failure
  modes:
    - anime entries nest under "show", not "anime" (wrong key = silently
      empty anime library, and an exclusion gap nothing would notice)
    - browse filters silently widen to "all" on an unknown segment and still
      return 200 — so unknown values are rejected rather than sent
    - TMDB IDs aren't unique across movie/TV; slugs aren't unique either
      (three Supermans), so links are built from the Simkl ID
    - /redirect must not be followed; the Location header is the whole answer
    - history rows with no Simkl ID are counted and returned, not dropped

re-com-video
  wikidata.py       maker signal — verified live, not just mocked
  scripts/probe.py  Phase 0 measurement
  24 tests.

  Cast is defined but deliberately NOT a default signal until billed-principal
  ordering is measured. maker_neighbours caps what one person contributes,
  because re-com's longest-running defect was an artist-centric graph where
  two seeds sharing an artist returned nearly the same results.

WHAT THE PROBE FOUND  (20 seeds, Wikidata half only — needs no key)
=============================================================================

1. THE GEOGRAPHY WORRY WAS THE WRONG WORRY.
   20/20 seeds resolved by IMDb ID, all with an original language. Indian
   seeds returned a *higher* median of maker neighbours (19) than Western
   (13.5). Wikidata is not weak on this catalogue.

2. THE REAL GAP IS FORMAT, AND IT'S NEW.
   Every seed returning zero maker neighbours is a series — Kota Factory 0,
   The Bear 0, Panchayat 1. Succession has no director in Wikidata at all and
   survives only on its composer. P57/P58 are film properties; series credit
   episode directors, who aren't modelled the same way.

   >> The maker signal is a MOVIE signal. For TV it degrades to composer-only,
   >> and sometimes to nothing. That makes Simkl's users_recommendations very
   >> likely the ONLY signal for TV — which RAISES the stakes on the one
   >> measurement still blocked. If TV neighbour counts also come back thin,
   >> v1 has no TV recommender, and the plan should say so plainly rather
   >> than shipping a thin one.

3. MUSIC DIRECTOR CARRIES INDIAN FILM — more than the plan guessed.
   composer (P86) fires on 6 of 9 Indian seeds and is usually the largest
   contributor: 12 of 23 for 12th Fail, 12 of 14 for Laapataa Ladies.

4. writer (P58) IS SPARSE — nothing for 5 of 9 Indian seeds. Consistent with
   the 22% writer coverage measured in §4.3. Earns a place in the signal list
   but not weight.

5. SAME-DIRECTOR CONCENTRATION is real but tolerable so far.
   Gangs of Wasseypur <-> Sacred Games (both Anurag Kashyap) share 10
   candidate titles, Jaccard 0.312, at a cap of 12 per person. Below the 0.5
   line. Re-measure with the viewer signal mixed in — this is the defect that
   cost re-com §7.10–§7.14 the most time.

6. TITLES COLLIDE exactly as §8.5 claimed. A live label query matched
   "Panchayat" to 4 Wikidata items, "Parasite" to 6, "Severance" to 5, "The
   Family Man" to 6. Nothing joins on anything but an IMDb ID.

Note: the probe reports the viewer-vs-maker gate as "undecided, not passed"
when the Simkl half hasn't run, rather than drawing a confident conclusion
from an empty set.

Full numbers: PLAN.md §9.1a.

THE ONE THING BLOCKING THE REST
=============================================================================

Register a free app at https://simkl.com/settings/developer
(PIN flow needs no client_secret, so any redirect URI works.)

Then:
    export SIMKL_CLIENT_ID=...
    python scripts/probe.py            # the decisive measurement

Worth doing alongside: request the Netflix personal-data export NOW — it takes
up to 30 days and Phase 3 wants it.

Also: scripts/seeds.json currently holds the plan's spot-check titles as
placeholders so the probe runs today. They're a stand-in. The probe measures
how the signals behave on YOUR catalogue, so it wants 20 titles you've
actually watched before the full run means anything.
